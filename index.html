Flamingo World

Explore the amazing life of these elegant birds.

Physical Appearance

Flamingos are known for their beautiful feathers and long legs.

Natural Habitat

Flamingos live in lakes, lagoons, and wetlands.

Diet and Nutrition

Flamingos feed on algae and small aquatic organisms.

Social Behavior

Flamingos are social birds that live in large groups.

Did You Know?

Flamingos get their pink color from pigments in their food.

Interesting Flamingo Facts

Flamingos have long legs and beautiful pink feathers.

They are elegant and amazing birds.

One interesting fact: Flamingos are born with gray feathers!

Flamingos belong to the family Phoenicopteridae.

Old information: Flamingos are white birds.

Updated information: Flamingos are famous for their pink color.
